RoyalSpice – Restaurant Website

Project Overview

RoyalSpice is a restaurant website developed for a client to establish a professional online presence for their restaurant. The website is designed to showcase the restaurant's identity, food offerings, and dining experience through an attractive, modern, and user-friendly interface.

The main objective of this project is to help customers easily explore the restaurant, discover its menu, learn more about its services, and connect with the restaurant.

Key Features

- Home Page: An attractive landing page highlighting the restaurant's brand, signature dishes, and overall dining experience.
- Menu Section: Displays food items with their names, descriptions, prices, and images.
- About Us: Introduces the restaurant, its story, and its specialties.
- Contact Section: Provides restaurant contact information and relevant location details.
- Responsive Design: A user-friendly layout designed to work across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop devices.
- Easy Navigation: A clean and intuitive interface that helps visitors find information quickly.

Project Objective

The goal of RoyalSpice is to strengthen the restaurant's digital presence, improve customer accessibility, present its food offerings effectively, and create a positive first impression for potential customers.
